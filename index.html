🌐 Agnostic Games — Accounts

This is the shared account service for the Agnostic Solutions games. It stores one login (username + 6-digit PIN, with optional email recovery) that works across every game — while each game keeps its own separate leaderboard.

There's nothing to see here. Head to a game to play and sign in.

API: /api/auth/* · /api/leaderboard · /api/sync
